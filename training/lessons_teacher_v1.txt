# --- definitions ---
what is a fact : a fact is a claim that can be checked and shown true .
what is a guess : a guess is a claim made without enough evidence to check it .
what is evidence : evidence is an observation or record that supports or refutes a claim .
what is a definition : a definition says what a word means , clearly enough to use it .
what is a cause : a cause is what makes something happen .
what is an effect : an effect is what happens because of a cause .
what is a plan : a plan is an ordered list of steps that reaches a goal .
what is a goal : a goal is a result someone has decided to work toward .
what is a customer : a customer is a person or business that buys goods or services .
what is an invoice : an invoice is a written request for payment for goods or services .
what is profit : profit is what remains when revenue is greater than costs .
what is a loss : a business loss is when costs are greater than revenue .
what is revenue : revenue is the money a business receives from selling .
what is a cost : a cost is money spent to run a business or make a product .
what is a deadline : a deadline is the latest time by which work must be done .
what is a promise : a promise is a commitment that creates an obligation to act .
what is trust : trust is earned by doing what you said , repeatedly , over time .
what is a record : a record is a stored account of what happened , kept to be checked later .
what is a summary : a summary restates the main points of something in fewer words .
what is a question : a question is a request for information that the asker does not yet have .
# --- counterexamples and corrections ---
is a guess a fact : no , a guess is not a fact , because a guess cannot yet be checked .
is silence consent : no , silence is not consent , consent must be given clearly .
does bigger mean better : no , bigger does not mean better , fit for the purpose is what matters .
is confidence proof : no , confidence is not proof , proof requires evidence .
can one example prove all : no , one example cannot prove a claim about all cases .
is correlation causation : no , two things moving together does not prove one caused the other .
should you trust one source : no , an important claim should be checked against more than one source .
# --- logic ---
if all a are b : and this is a , then this is b .
if no a are b : and this is a , then this is not b .
what does if mean : if introduces a condition , the result follows only when the condition holds .
what is true : true describes a claim that matches the facts .
what is false : false describes a claim that does not match the facts .
what is a contradiction : a contradiction is two claims that cannot both be true at once .
what follows from a and b : from a and b together , both a is true and b is true .
# --- how to work: verifiable procedures ---
how do you check work : compare the result against the requirement , step by step , and record the check .
how do you solve a problem : state the problem , list what is known , choose steps , act , then verify .
how do you answer a customer : answer clearly , confirm the facts first , and promise only what you can do .
how do you handle a mistake : admit the mistake , record it , fix what can be fixed , and say what changed .
how do you prioritize tasks : do first what is urgent and important , and schedule the rest honestly .
how do you learn something new : find a reliable source , practice on examples , and test yourself .
what do you do before acting : check the facts , weigh the risk , and get approval when the action is risky .
what do you do after acting : record what happened , verify the result , and report it plainly .
why verify after acting : because an action is not done until its result has been checked .
why keep records : records let anyone check later what happened , when , and why .
